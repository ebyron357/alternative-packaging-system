### ⚠️ FLAG-01 — LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED: Ingredient Terminology
The panel reads "Hemp Derived THC."

The ALT Syrup (separate product) reads "100% Hemp-Derived Delta 9 THC Distillate."

Regulatory landscape:
- The 2018 Farm Bill defines hemp as Cannabis sativa with ≤0.3% Delta-9 THC on dry weight basis.
- Some states require "Delta-9 THC" specifically to distinguish from Delta-8 or other cannabinoids.
- The term "Hemp Derived THC" without specifying Delta-9 may create ambiguity in certain state markets.
- The syrup panel references ".3% on a dry basis" — confirms Farm Bill compliance intent.

**Do not finalize the ingredient list copy for the label until legal counsel confirms exact regulated language for each distribution state.**
