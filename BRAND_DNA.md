## BRAND IDENTITY

ALTERNATIVE™ is not simply a beverage. It is an identity.

Every interaction with the brand should reinforce confidence, sophistication, and modern living.

The customer should feel elevated before opening the can. The package begins that experience.

**Tagline:** A NEW STATE OF MIND

---

## BRAND PERSONALITY

If ALTERNATIVE™ were a person, it would be:

- Confident without arrogance
- Sophisticated without pretension
- Modern without chasing trends
- Intelligent without overexplaining
- Premium without feeling inaccessible
- Calm under pressure
- Social without being loud
- Refined without being sterile
- Memorable without demanding attention

---

## WHAT THE BRAND COMMUNICATES

Every design decision should reinforce these qualities:

Elevated. Intentional. Premium. Modern. Clean. Architectural. Editorial. Adult. Confident. Timeless.

The brand should feel established even if someone has never seen it before.

---

## WHAT THE BRAND NEVER COMMUNICATES

Never allow ALTERNATIVE™ to feel:

Cheap. Loud. Trendy. Juvenile. Cannabis-first. CBD-first. Psychedelic. Medical. Supplement-like.
Smoke shop. Convenience-store generic. Artificial. Over-designed. Cluttered.

## COLOR SYSTEM — LOCKED

### Primary: Matte Black
- Represents: Confidence, Premium quality, Sophistication, Timelessness
- Rules:
  - Solid matte appearance only
  - No gradients
  - No gloss effects
  - No carbon fiber textures
  - No camouflage
  - No visual noise
  - Matte Black is never replaced by flavor colors

### Accent: Metallic Gold
- Represents: Craftsmanship, Refinement, Prestige
- Used for: Hero A, premium hierarchy, divider lines, premium emphasis
- Rules:
  - Gold communicates craftsmanship — never decoration
  - Gold is used sparingly and deliberately
  - If adding more Gold does not improve hierarchy, remove it
  - Never excessive

### Secondary: Warm White
- Represents: Clarity, Readability, Trust
- Used for: All typography, product information, compliance text
- Rules:
  - Never use harsh blue-white
  - Never use gray body text
  - Maintain excellent contrast against Matte Black

### Flavor Colors
- Flavor is secondary. The brand is primary.
- Flavor differentiation should be subtle
- Never allow flavor color to overpower the Matte Black + Gold identity

## HERO A — SACRED BRAND ELEMENT

The Hero A is the most recognizable symbol of ALTERNATIVE™.

It represents: Confidence, Elevation, Simplicity, Recognition.

**Rules — absolute:**
- It is never decorative
- It is never stretched
- It is never rotated
- It is never modified
- It is never recreated
- Only the approved vector may be used
- The Hero A should dominate the front panel without overwhelming typography
- Its size should create immediate recognition from approximately 10 feet away
- Generous clear space around the Hero A at all times
- Typography supports the Hero A — never competes with it

---

## WORDMARK — ALTERNATIVE™

- Never feels generic — feels established
- Carries authority without becoming oversized
- The relationship between Hero A and ALTERNATIVE™ creates immediate recognition
- Never distorted
- Never artificially condensed or expanded
- Only approved artwork may be used

## TYPOGRAPHY SYSTEM

Typography is the strongest luxury signal on this label.

### Principles
- Editorial. Architectural. Balanced. Intentional. Premium.
- Typography carries the emotion — not decorative graphics, not unnecessary icons
- Every letter should feel intentionally placed
- Typography should communicate luxury before the customer consciously notices it

## DESIGN PROHIBITION LIST

The following must never appear on any ALTERNATIVE™ label:
- Cannabis leaves or cannabis imagery
- Fruit illustrations or photographs
- Decorative icons (only compliance/recycling/mandatory regulatory icons permitted)
- Photography of any kind
- Gradients on the Matte Black field
- Gloss effects on the primary surface
- Any visual element that resembles cannabis, CBD, supplement, energy drink, or smoke shop packaging
- AI-generated artwork that is visually generic or template-based

---

## SURFACE TREATMENT

- Base finish: Soft matte (the can should feel premium before it is read)
- Metallic Gold elements: Metallic ink or foil (to be confirmed with Canworks — Risk R-15)
- The package should appear premium before being touched

## DESIGN REVIEW FILTER

Before approving any design decision, ask:

1. Does this strengthen ALTERNATIVE™?
2. Does this increase premium perception?
3. Does this improve shelf recognition?
4. Does this build long-term brand equity?
5. Would this still feel appropriate ten years from now?
6. Would this compete beside the finest premium beverages in the country?
7. Would a national retailer proudly stock it?
8. Would someone notice it from 10 feet away?
9. Can anything be removed to make it stronger?
10. Is every element earning its place?

**If any answer is "No" — refine the solution.**
