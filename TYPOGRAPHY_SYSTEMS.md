## BRIEF

ALTERNATIVE™ requires a premium editorial sans-serif typography system that:

- Communicates confidence, sophistication, and modern luxury
- Reads as editorial and architectural — not cannabis, supplement, energy drink, or technology
- Performs exceptionally at display sizes (Hero A relationship, wordmark hierarchy)
- Remains fully legible at 6pt compliance sizes on a printed can
- Carries sufficient weight range to serve every level of the 8-level hierarchy
- Is commercially licensable for print production

Five systems are presented. Each includes display and body/compliance usage, rationale, concerns, and notable brand context.

## SYSTEM 1 — SÖHNE
**Foundry:** Klim Type Foundry (New Zealand)
**Classification:** Warm modernist grotesque

### What it is
Söhne is Klim's interpretation of Akzidenz-Grotesk as seen through the lens of mid-century German modernism. It is what premium sans-serif typography looks and feels like when executed without compromise. The name means "sons" in German — it carries historical weight while remaining contemporary.

### Why it fits ALTERNATIVE™
Söhne communicates confidence without aggression. It has warmth that prevents it from reading as cold or corporate. At display sizes it commands attention without shouting. At small sizes its generous x-height and open apertures maintain exceptional legibility. It does not remind anyone of cannabis packaging, supplements, or energy drinks. It reminds people of fine editorial design.

### Hierarchy application
| Level | Content | Weight |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | A NEW STATE OF MIND | Söhne Leicht (Light) — tracked wide |
| 2 | Hero A | N/A — vector mark |
| 3 | ALTERNATIVE™ | Söhne Halbfett (Semibold) or Söhne Kräftig (Bold) |
| 4 | SESSION™ | Söhne Buch (Book) or Leicht |
| 5 | 5MG THC PER CAN | Söhne Leicht — tracked |
| 6 | HEMP-DERIVED THC BEVERAGE | Söhne Leicht — tracked |
| 7 | PASSION FRUIT | Söhne Buch |
| 8 | 12 FL OZ (355 mL) | Söhne Leicht |
| Compliance | All body/legal text | Söhne Buch or Leicht |

### Signal
Reads as: premium European editorial. Used by: high-end publications, luxury goods brands, premium food and beverage.

### Concern
Klim typefaces carry a premium reputation that may make them instantly recognizable to design-aware consumers — which in this context is a positive signal, not a risk.

## SYSTEM 2 — FOUNDERS GROTESK
**Foundry:** Klim Type Foundry (New Zealand)
**Classification:** Early 20th century grotesque revival

### What it is
Founders Grotesk is drawn from nineteenth and early twentieth century grotesque types found in specimen books from Stephenson Blake, Schriftgießerei Schelter & Giesecke, and the Central Type Foundry. It carries the authority of historical grotesque design with the technical precision of contemporary type production.

### Why it fits ALTERNATIVE™
Where Söhne reads as warm and editorial, Founders Grotesk reads as established and authoritative. It communicates that the brand has always existed — that it was here before you noticed it. This is exactly the quality the Brand Bible describes: "The brand should feel established even if someone has never seen it before." At display size it has a quiet confidence that Söhne's rationalism doesn't offer. At small sizes it is exceptionally legible.

### Hierarchy application
| Level | Content | Weight |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | A NEW STATE OF MIND | Founders Grotesk X-Condensed Light — tracked |
| 2 | Hero A | N/A — vector mark |
| 3 | ALTERNATIVE™ | Founders Grotesk Semibold |
| 4 | SESSION™ | Founders Grotesk Light |
| 5 | 5MG THC PER CAN | Founders Grotesk X-Condensed Light |
| 6 | HEMP-DERIVED THC BEVERAGE | Founders Grotesk X-Condensed Light |
| 7 | PASSION FRUIT | Founders Grotesk Regular |
| 8 | 12 FL OZ (355 mL) | Founders Grotesk Light |
| Compliance | All body/legal text | Founders Grotesk Regular |

### Signal
Reads as: established luxury. Used by: premium spirits brands, high-end retail, luxury consumer goods.

### Concern
The X-Condensed styles offer powerful hierarchy differentiation at multiple scale levels — valuable on a sleek can where horizontal space is limited. This is a strength, not a risk.

## SYSTEM 3 — GT AMERICA
**Foundry:** Grilli Type (Switzerland)
**Classification:** Hybrid grotesque-geometric

### What it is
GT America bridges the tradition of American Grotesque typography (Bureau Grot, Franklin Gothic) with the precision of Swiss grotesque design (Helvetica, Neue Haas Grotesk). It sits at the intersection of warmth and precision — neither fully geometric nor fully humanist.

### Why it fits ALTERNATIVE™
GT America communicates modern American premium. It is confident at large sizes and generous at small sizes. The extended family (six widths, eight weights) provides every hierarchy tool needed across eight levels. It reads as intentional and designed without calling attention to the typeface itself — exactly what premium brand typography should do.

### Hierarchy application
| Level | Content | Weight |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | A NEW STATE OF MIND | GT America Compressed Light — tracked |
| 2 | Hero A | N/A — vector mark |
| 3 | ALTERNATIVE™ | GT America Standard Bold |
| 4 | SESSION™ | GT America Standard Regular |
| 5 | 5MG THC PER CAN | GT America Compressed Light |
| 6 | HEMP-DERIVED THC BEVERAGE | GT America Compressed Light |
| 7 | PASSION FRUIT | GT America Standard Regular |
| 8 | 12 FL OZ (355 mL) | GT America Standard Light |
| Compliance | All body/legal text | GT America Standard Regular |

### Signal
Reads as: premium American editorial. Used by: luxury lifestyle brands, premium consumer products, high-end food and beverage.

### Concern
GT America is more widely deployed than Söhne or Founders Grotesk, which means it is slightly less distinctive. However, execution quality always differentiates — a system is only as common as the work done with it.

## SYSTEM 4 — AKTIV GROTESK
**Foundry:** Dalton Maag (London)
**Classification:** Contemporary grotesque

### What it is
Aktiv Grotesk was designed to be a contemporary alternative to Helvetica — more considered in its details, more versatile across sizes, and specifically engineered for legibility across every output condition. It is widely available and commercially proven at scale.

### Why it fits ALTERNATIVE™
Aktiv Grotesk's primary strength is its exceptional performance at small sizes — critical for compliance copy on a printed can. It reads as premium and professional without being cold. Its neutrality is an asset: it puts the brand identity (Hero A, hierarchy, composition) forward rather than the typeface itself. It is the most commercially battle-tested option in this list.

### Hierarchy application

| 7 | PASSION FRUIT | Aktiv Grotesk Regular |
| 8 | 12 FL OZ (355 mL) | Aktiv Grotesk Light |
| Compliance | All body/legal text | Aktiv Grotesk Regular |

### Concern

## SYSTEM 5 — SUISSE INT'L
**Foundry:** Swiss Typefaces (Switzerland)
**Classification:** Swiss modernist grotesque

### What it is
Suisse Int'l is a contemporary interpretation of Swiss modernist grotesque typography — the tradition that produced Helvetica and Univers — executed with modern type production standards. It is precise, structured, and uncompromising. The name itself signals the tradition it inhabits.

### Why it fits ALTERNATIVE™
Suisse Int'l communicates architectural precision and international premium quality. It is the typographic equivalent of a well-designed Swiss watch — no element present that is not necessary, every element in the correct relationship. On a Matte Black can with Metallic Gold accents, Suisse Int'l in Warm White would read as quietly powerful. Its strict rationalism ensures the Hero A and the Matte Black field carry the personality while the type carries the authority.

### Hierarchy application
| Level | Content | Weight |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | A NEW STATE OF MIND | Suisse Int'l Ultralight — tracked wide |
| 2 | Hero A | N/A — vector mark |
| 3 | ALTERNATIVE™ | Suisse Int'l SemiBold or Bold |
| 4 | SESSION™ | Suisse Int'l Thin or Light |
| 5 | 5MG THC PER CAN | Suisse Int'l Ultralight — tracked |
| 6 | HEMP-DERIVED THC BEVERAGE | Suisse Int'l Ultralight — tracked |
| 7 | PASSION FRUIT | Suisse Int'l Regular |
| 8 | 12 FL OZ (355 mL) | Suisse Int'l Ultralight |
| Compliance | All body/legal text | Suisse Int'l Regular |

### Signal
Reads as: Swiss precision, international luxury, architectural confidence. Used by: premium design studios, luxury brands, high-end beverage and spirits.

### Concern

## PROGRAM DIRECTOR RECOMMENDATION

**Primary recommendation: Söhne or Founders Grotesk**

Both are from Klim Type Foundry — arguably the most respected independent type foundry in the world. Both carry a premium signal that is immediately legible to design-aware consumers while remaining invisible to general consumers (meaning it communicates premium without announcing itself).

- **Choose Söhne** if the brand direction is warm, editorial, and approachable within its premium positioning.
- **Choose Founders Grotesk** if the brand direction is authoritative, established, and historically grounded.

Both are the correct choice for a brand that intends to become the benchmark for premium elevated beverages. Both are wrong for cannabis packaging, supplement branding, or energy drinks — which is exactly the point.

**Secondary recommendation: Suisse Int'l**
If the brand direction is maximum architectural precision and Swiss modernist confidence, Suisse Int'l is the correct choice.
